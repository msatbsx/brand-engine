# Aagee Logo Usage

## Logo Versions

### Primary logo
Combines the custom "A" symbol with the word "agee". Use for most brand contexts: websites, print, marketing campaigns.

### Symbol logo
The custom "A" mark only. Use for app icons, social media profiles, favicons, and branded merchandise where space is limited.

## Logo Proportions

### Primary logo grid
- Total width: 30.7x units
- Symbol portion: 7.5x wide, 8.5x tall
- Gap between symbol and wordmark: 0.5x
- Wordmark height: 6x (with 2.5x space above and below)

### Symbol logo grid
- Width: 7.5x units
- Height: 8.5x units

## Clear Space

The clear space protects the logo from other graphic elements, text, or edges that could cause visual clutter. Space is calculated by the height of the brand symbol.

- **Primary logo**: clear space of 0.5X on all sides, where X is the height of the logo
- **Symbol logo**: clear space of 0.5A on all sides, where A is the height of the symbol

## Minimum Size

- **Primary logo**: must be at least 75 pixels wide
- **Symbol logo**: must be at least 25 pixels wide

## Recommended Logo Background Combinations

To maintain brand clarity and legibility, place the Aagee logo only on backgrounds that provide strong contrast.

**Recommended:**
- Black logo on Lime (#DDFB66) background
- Lime logo on Black (#111111) background
- Lime logo on Midnight Navy (#131F30) background
- Black logo on White (#FFFFFF) background — for print/documentation
- White logo on Black (#111111) background — for print/documentation

Use the Lime–Black or Lime–Midnight Navy combinations to reflect the brand's identity. Use black and white versions for printing or documentation.

**Prohibited background combinations:**
- Lime logo on White — insufficient contrast
- Mint logo on White — insufficient contrast
- Sky logo on White — insufficient contrast
- Dark Grey on Midnight Navy — insufficient contrast
- Mint on Lime — too similar
- Sky on Lime — too similar
- Sky on Mint — too similar
- Mint on Sky — too similar
- Black on Midnight Navy — insufficient contrast
- Accent colour backgrounds (Mint, Sky) — avoid for logo placement

## Prohibited Logo Uses

To keep the Aagee logo consistent and recognisable, avoid the following:

1. Do not stretch horizontally
2. Do not stretch vertically
3. Do not change colours of any element
4. Do not rotate or distort
5. Do not adjust opacity
6. Do not add shadows or effects
7. Do not add outlines or any decorations
8. Do not add outline strokes to the logo
9. Do not place the symbol above the logotype
