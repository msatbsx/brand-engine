## 1. Logo (Page 4)

### 1.1 Logo Description

The Aagee logo is made up of two parts: the bold letter "A" and the word "agee", combined into one strong brand name. The "A" is the main symbol, and if looked at closely, there's a hidden "G" inside. Its triangular shape points upward, representing growth, new ideas, and forward movement. The sharp angles and dynamic lines in the design create a sense of speed and transformation, which reflects the world of technology — always shifting, always improving. Altogether, Aagee presents itself as a modern, forward-thinking brand that's ready to grow, adapt, and thrive in the fast-moving tech world.

---

### 1.2 Primary Logo

The primary logo combines the custom letter "A" with the word "agee" to form the full brand name — Aagee.

This version is designed for most brand uses, including websites, printed materials, and marketing campaigns, where the full name needs to be clearly seen and recognized.

**Versions:**
- Primary logo on **lime/yellow-green background** — logo in **black**
- Primary logo on **black background** — logo in **lime/yellow-green**

---

### 1.3 Symbol Logo

The symbol logo is a simplified version that focuses only on the custom "A".

It is perfect for smaller spaces or digital applications, such as app icons, social media profiles, favicons, or branded merchandise where a compact mark is more effective than the full name.

**Versions:**
- Symbol logo on **lime/yellow-green background** — symbol in **black**
- Symbol logo on **black background** — symbol in **lime/yellow-green**

---

### 1.7 Logo Background Control

#### Recommended Colour Combinations

| Combination Name | Logo Colour | Background Colour |
|---|---|---|
| Black / Lime | Black | Lime `#DDFB66` |
| Lime / Black | Lime `#DDFB66` | Black `#111111` |
| Lime / Midnight Navy | Lime `#DDFB66` | Midnight Navy `#131F30` |

#### Black & White Combinations

| Combination Name | Logo Colour | Background Colour |
|---|---|---|
| Black / White | Black | White |
| White / Black | White | Black |

**Notes:**
- Lime–Black or Lime–Midnight Navy combinations are recommended to reflect the brand's identity.
- Black and white versions are for printing or documentation.
- Avoid using background colours that lack contrast or using accent colour backgrounds.

#### Prohibited Background Combinations

- Lime logo on a white background (insufficient contrast)
- Black logo on a lime/green background
- Black logo on a light blue background

---

### 1.8 Prohibited Logo Usage

To keep the Aagee logo consistent and recognizable, avoid the following:

1. Do not stretch horizontally.
2. Do not stretch vertically.
3. Do not change colors of any element.
4. Do not rotate or distort.
5. Do not adjust opacity.
6. Do not add shadows or effects.
7. Do not add outlines or any decorations.
8. Do not add outline strokes to the logo.
9. Do not place the symbol above the logotype.

## 2. Color (Page 12)

### 2.1 Color Palette

The Aagee color system highlights **Lime** as the primary color. Supporting colors **Mint** and **Sky** bring freshness and visual engagement, while neutral tones — **White**, **Black**, and **Dark Grey** — create a high-contrast background.

Use Lime as the focus, with Mint and Sky as accents, and neutrals for backgrounds and text.

| Color Name | Hex Code |
|---|---|
| White | `#FFFFFF` |
| Midnight Navy | `#131F30` |
| Black | `#111111` |
| Sky | `#83C6ED` |
| Dark Grey | `#1A1A1A` |
| Mint | `#9CEE95` |
| Lime | `#DDFB66` |

---

### 2.2 Suggested Color Schemes

The following combinations are recommended for their successful high-contrast performance.

| Background | Foreground |
|---|---|
| Dark Grey `#1A1A1A` | Lime `#DDFB66` |
| Midnight Navy `#131F30` | Lime `#DDFB66` |
| Lime `#DDFB66` | Midnight Navy `#131F30` |
| Dark Grey `#1A1A1A` | Mint `#9CEE95` |
| Mint `#9CEE95` | Dark Grey `#1A1A1A` |
| Dark Grey `#1A1A1A` | Sky `#83C6ED` |
| Sky `#83C6ED` | Dark Grey `#1A1A1A` |

---

### 2.3 Combinations to Avoid

Do not use color combinations that create legibility issues with text or graphic elements.

| Combination to Avoid |
|---|
| Lime on White |
| Mint on White |
| Sky on White |
| Dark Grey on Black |
| Mint on Lime |
| Sky on Lime |
| Sky on Mint |
| Mint on Sky |
| Black on Midnight Navy |

---

### 2.4 Color Gradients

Gradients can be created using Lime, Mint, and Sky colors, applied at a **45° diagonal angle** to create a soft and smooth transition.

Use gradients as backgrounds, but always prioritize contrast and readability when layering text or graphic elements.

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Angle | 45° |
| Permitted gradient colors | Lime `#DDFB66`, Mint `#9CEE95`, Sky `#83C6ED` |

**Gradient examples:**
- Lime-based gradient at 45°
- Mint-based gradient at 45°
- Sky-based gradient at 45°

## 3. Typography (Page 18)

### 3.1 Primary Typeface: Figtree

Figtree is the primary typeface for all presentations, brand marketing, and landing pages. Four weights are used, from Regular to Bold, to support flexible use cases.

| Weight | Usage |
|---|---|
| Figtree Regular | Body copy |
| Figtree Medium | Body copy |
| Figtree SemiBold | Headline copy |
| Figtree Bold | Headline copy |

**Character set:**

### 3.2 Secondary Typeface: Fraunces

Fraunces is the secondary typeface. Usage is limited. It helps highlight small moments of engagement, such as supporting text elements or decorative graphic banners. Two weights are used.

## 4. Graphic Elements (Page 21)

### 4.1 Logo Shape

The "A" shape can be extracted and broken down into smaller components to add visual interest across various brand applications.

**Rules:**
- Elements can be cropped, scaled, rotated, or recoloured (using brand colours only).
- Maintain clarity and simplicity to ensure the shapes remain visually connected to the original logo.

---

### 4.2 The Rubik's Cube Grid

The "A" shape is built using a Rubik's Cube-inspired grid. The grid helps keep everything consistent while still allowing room to be creative.

**Rules:**
- Only the three visible faces of the cube (as shown in the diagram) are used to develop illustrations.
- The grid can be used to create unique and playful graphic patterns.
