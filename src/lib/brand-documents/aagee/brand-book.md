# Aagee Brand Book

## Brand Overview

Aagee is a modern, forward-thinking brand that presents itself as ready to grow, adapt, and thrive in the fast-moving tech world.

## Logo

The Aagee logo is made up of two parts: the bold letter "A" and the word "agee", combined into one strong brand name. The "A" is the main symbol, and if looked at closely, there's a hidden "G" inside. Its triangular shape points upward, representing growth, new ideas, and forward movement. The sharp angles and dynamic lines in the design create a sense of speed and transformation, which reflects the world of technology, always shifting, always improving.

### Logo versions

**Primary logo** — combines the custom "A" symbol with the word "agee" to form the full brand name. Designed for most brand uses including websites, printed materials, and marketing campaigns where the full name needs to be clearly seen and recognised.

**Symbol logo** — a simplified version that focuses only on the custom "A". Perfect for smaller spaces or digital applications such as app icons, social media profiles, favicons, or branded merchandise where a compact mark is more effective than the full name.

## Colour System

The Aagee colour system highlights Lime as the primary colour. Supporting colours Mint and Sky bring freshness and visual engagement, while neutral tones like White, Black, and Dark Grey create a high-contrast background.

Use Lime as the focus, with Mint and Sky as accents, and neutrals for backgrounds and text.

## Typography

Figtree is the primary typeface for all presentations, brand marketing, and landing pages.

Four weights are used:
- **Figtree Regular** — body copy
- **Figtree Medium** — body copy (emphasis)
- **Figtree SemiBold** — headline copy
- **Figtree Bold** — headline copy (primary)

Figtree Bold and SemiBold are used for headline copy. Regular and Medium are used for body copy.
