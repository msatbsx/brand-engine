### Our Verbs (Page 13)

We are a dynamic brand and our proactive language always communicates a committed sense of purpose and optimism.

**WE PARTNER**
We speak as collaborators who empathize with our clients and are ready to join them on their journey of transformation. We emphasize that we understand the challenges of fundamental change while advocating for the boldness to choose it.

**WE REWIRE**
We ask clients to reimagine their business in order to reinvent it. We call the process 'rewiring.' Our offering is never theoretical or ambiguous, it's based on a deep analysis of every client's unique challenges using all of our real-world experience.

**WE ORCHESTRATE**
We help our customers understand that there is hidden harmony in their workflows and their operating models that we can help unlock. We talk about Intelligent Orchestration as a liberating process that unifies their business into one seamless, efficient flow.

**WE TRANSFORM**
We are agents of change and we are laser-focused on genuine transformation to accelerate efficiency and growth for the future.

**WE DELIVER**
We are not technologists, we are business consultants and we are always focused on outcomes and realizing value. We never talk about AI-native transformation without its real-world benefits.

### Visionary Marketing (Page 14)

When the brand is in the spotlight for marketing purposes—whether that's at physical events, in advertising or on our own homepage—we use an optimistic, pioneering tone that sets out our transformative vision.

Our headlines are a clarion call for a new way of doing business and use bold statements and inspiring metaphors for light, flow and orchestration to drive our visionary approach.

#### Example Headlines:

- The age of Intelligent Orchestration is here.
- See business in a new light.
- Confront complexity. Then dissolve it.
- Every atom of your business in its right place.
- When everything flows together it knows together.
- Unleash brilliance.

### Conversational Social (Page 15)

When the brand shows up in social channels like LinkedIn, we couch our industry-leading vision in a more conversational way much like a face-to-face meeting with clients and customers.

Our social content relies more on valuable business intelligence like data and case studies than it does on the aspirational claims of our marketing content. Here we are conversational, approachable, informal but always based on serious thought leadership.

#### Example LinkedIn Post (shown):

**Cprime, Inc** — 57,838 followers

> "Our latest report on **Real-Time Responsiveness in Customer Experience** is published today. You can download it directly from the Cprime website or, even better, drop by to see us at **IPPE** for a first-hand glimpse into the immediacy of ecommerce in the age of Intelligent Orchestration. We're excited to see how much impact this is having on our clients and we think you will be too. Come down and see your business in a new light."

**Hashtags used:** #IntelligentOrchestration #BusinessInnovation #AITransformation

### Compelling Consulting (Page 16)

Our business documents should reflect the brand in their clarity and their boldness. Writing about business technology in general and AI in particular can be complex, dense and technical so we always work hard to convey that information in the most compelling and digestible way possible.

We avoid using jargon where a simpler vocabulary will work. We avoid long, run-on sentences, breaking them into shorter units and lists wherever possible. We introduce and summarise our documents in warm, conversational tones. We are aspirational but always validated by data.

#### Rules:

**DON'T USE LONG SENTENCES**
*(shown in greyed-out/muted treatment to indicate incorrect usage)*
> Enterprise Technology, Strategic Portfolio and Finance Management solutions align technology investments with key transformative initiatives, optimizing operations in a cloud-driven business environment and enabling organizations to achieve sustainable growth, enhance decision-making, and maintain competitiveness.

**DO USE SHORTER SENTENCES & LESS ADJECTIVES**
> Enterprise Technology, Strategic Portfolio and Finance Management solutions align technology investments with key transformative initiatives. This alignment optimizes operations in a cloud-driven business environment and enables organizations to improve growth, decision-making, and competitiveness.

**SPEAK AS "WE"**
Cprime is a thought leader in the industry so it's good to use "We" liberally and center our POV where relevant. This makes the brand feel present and proactive.
- *Example:* We are seeing AI-first companies—the OpenAIs, Tiktoks and Runways of the world—challenge incumbents in ways previously unimaginable.

**BE ASPIRATIONAL**
Don't be frightened to be aspirational. AI is a transformative technology and Cprime is a visionary brand. We are not exaggerating our future impact so you should feel comfortable using ambitious language in order to communicate. Remember to use our dynamic verbs (see previous slide) where applicable to emphasise our assertiveness and game-changing potential.
- *Example:* Immediacy reimagines experience and redefines efficiency.

**BE APPROPRIATELY CONVERSATIONAL**
Our pitches and presentations are always serious and business-like but there is a place for more direct conversational language in summarizing them. This doesn't mean oversimplifying but rather shaping persuasive arguments in a way that connects.
- *Example:* You can brilliantly execute in a low growth market, or moderately execute in a high growth market. You guess which scenario outperforms the others.

**BE OPTIMISTIC**
It's of paramount importance that we convey our strong belief in a better future with better outcomes through the use of AI and Intelligent Orchestration. Look for opportunities to convey that optimism throughout our communications and especially in headlines.
- *Example:* Orchestrating a Brighter Future.

## BRAND LEXICON

> "There are a few key words that arise directly from our brand identity that can help us own a specific and ownable POV on AI transformation. These come from our prismatic visual identity and from a metaphorical way to talk about Intelligent Orchestration."

### FLOW
> The concept of flow refers to multiple points and systems working seamlessly in unison. We often visually compare this to natural systems of intelligence like flocks of birds, or mycelium, or atomic particles. Flow is a highly desirable state for a business to be in, meaning it works without friction and with every part of it moving in the same direction. Flow can explicitly mean 'workflow' but it can also be a way to describe the overall dynamic of a business above and beyond individual workflows. Flow is a direct outcome of Intelligent Orchestration.

### ILLUMINATION
> Crpime's visual brand arises from a prismatic metaphor and that means that light - and words associated with it - fit well with the brand. Illumination is a metaphor for the holistic understanding of a business that AI can generate. Implementing AI systems across a business will shed light on all the unseen complexity that can slow a company down.

### BRILLIANCE
> Another 'light' word, brilliance is powerfully positive word that communicates the power of Cprime to transform and our optimistic outlook. We say that implementing AI systems across a business will unleash brilliance. We mean that we are unlocking true potential and helping our customers shine.

### AI-NATIVE
> When we describe ourselves or systems of work as AI-native, we mean an enterprise in which AI is intrinsic and does not feature as simply an add-on or a feature. AI-native businesses have established AI at their core and everything they do flows from that.

### HARMONY
> When all of the instruments of an orchestra are playing together or when human voices sing together we can hear what it's like for individual elements to work together in beautiful synchrony. It's the same for business. If Intelligent Orchestration is the tool then harmony is the outcome. Harmony means super-efficient, frictionless ways of working and customer experiences that are as seamless as possible.

### ACCELERATE
> Cprime is helping companies accelerate into the future by fundamentally rewiring their workflows. Acceleration can refer to efficiency, growth or profit. It is always aimed towards the future where Cprime can help you become a leading light in your industry.

### ORCHESTRATE
> Orchestration obviously derives from Intelligent Orchestration, the key part of our transformation offering. Just like a conductor and his players, orchestration is the masterful manipulation of any number of disparate elements, bringing them together to work in unison.

### NEW LIGHT
> Cprime is bringing a new light to business transformation that allows us to look at things in a new way and to connect them differently with evolved workflows. 'New light' is both a new way of thinking and a new way of doing.

## PARTNER LOCKUPS (Page 26)

### Overview
- The Cprime logo can be paired with partner logos to showcase partnerships.
- Sizing and spacing consistency must adhere to these guidelines.
- The **x-height of the Cprime logotype** is used to determine proper spacing.
- The **width and height of the Cprime logo** are used as measuring devices to ensure partner logos appear proportionate to Cprime's.

### Horizontal Lockups
- **Center-align** with Cprime logotype.
- Partner logo: **no wider than 3 lengths** of the Cprime logo.
- Spacing between logos: **1x** (one x-height unit) + separator mark

### Square or Stacked Lockups
- **No taller than the Cprime logo** and **no wider than 2 lengths** of the logo.

### Vertical Lockups
- **No taller than 3X** and **no wider than 2 lengths** of the Cprime logo.

## LOGO COLOR (Page 27)

### Primary Rule
- The Cprime logo is primarily shown in **black and white**.
- In some instances, the **prism icon** can be shown in gradient color (see Prism Icon Color section).
- The **wordmark (Cprime letters) will always be black or white**.

### Color Placement Rules
| Background | Logo Color |
|---|---|
| Dark colors | White logo |
| Light colors | Black logo |
| Dark imagery | White logo — always place in area of high contrast |
| Light imagery | Black logo — always place in area of high contrast |

### Creating Contrast
- Minimum contrast ratio: **3:1**
- White logos on dark areas of images.
- Black logos on light areas of images.
- The Cprime logo should **not** be placed over busy or high-detail sections of an image where contrast is uneven or insufficient.
- For images where minimum contrast cannot be achieved naturally, use **overlays** (semi-transparent dark or light gradient) to create contrast.
- Alternatively, **adjust the brightness of the image**.

---

## PRISM ICON COLOR (Page 28)

### Rules
- In the majority of applications, the prism icon will be **black and white**.
- Gradient color in the prism icon is acceptable **when telling a more dynamic brand story through logo animation**.
- The prism icon can animate in **high-impact digital applications** when the Cprime logo appears large and in great detail.
- Gradient spectrums from the brand palette can **pulse and undulate** across the icon, much like ever-changing waves of light.

### Appropriate Applications for Animated Prism Icon
- Brand videos
- Conference presentations
- Interactive trade show booths
- Large-scale digital ad spaces (e.g., billboard or transit display)

### Placement Rules for Animated Logo
- When animating, the prism icon will pulse through different spectrums from brand gradients.
- For maximum contrast, the **animated logo must be placed on a white background**.
- When showcasing the animated logo on digital applications, the **logo must always appear very large**.

### Color Role Descriptions:

**Black**

Neutral gradients are just that — neutral. They soften and balance our prismatic palette and can be used more often, especially when we want to communicate less boldly.

**White**
White is our dominant background color. In layouts where text is placed on top of solid colors or dark gradients, white should be used as the text color.

## PRISMATIC MOTION PRINCIPLES (Page 79)

Motion design and animation come to life in very deliberate ways within the Cprime identity system.

There are three core motion attributes that enhance the meaning of our communications. These work together to tell a compelling story of our brand.

### Three Core Motion Attributes

#### 1. THE SUBTLE MOVEMENT OF LIGHT
Subtle moving gradients with pulses of prismatic light can be created to convey a sense of optimism and progress. These types of animations should be used as backgrounds for ideas on slides in presentations and films.

#### 2. PRISMATIC TRANSITIONS
In linear storytelling, we use bold animations to transition from one idea to the next. The motion should be quick, colorful, light-filled, and orchestrated. These transitions are used in executions such as brand films or cycling through ambient screens at a company event.

#### 3. INTELLIGENT ORCHESTRATION
We express intelligent orchestration through prismatic animations, but we also use this concept in other narrative communications to more explicitly highlight the benefits of working with Cprime.

---

## ANIMATION ELEMENTS (Page 80)

Animations are an ever-evolving and moving representation of the brand. They are a metaphor for dissolving complexity, intelligently orchestrating data, and real-time transformation.

Animations are built from a set of core attributes that create a consistency to keep brand recognition strong: core brand colors, basic geometric shapes (circles/squares) and patterns.

AI models also offer an opportunity to provide the variables that power the changes within our brand animations.

### Animation Element Categories

#### COLOR
- Animations may be shown in either one of these gradient spectrums. Please refer to the color section of the guidelines for color codes.
- Animations may also be shown in solid black or white.
- **Gradient spectrums shown:**
  - Yellow → Orange → Magenta/Pink gradient
  - Dark Green → Light Yellow-Green gradient

#### GEOMETRIC ELEMENTS
- The variety of patterns and forms that the logo takes on is made up of circles and squares.
- Those shapes can scale and multiply as much as needed within the boundaries.

#### PATTERN
- The logo will always take the form of a pattern.
- The patterns created from circles and squares can transition from simple to complex and from flat to dimensional.

## LOGO IN MOTION (Page 81)

In high-impact digital applications, our logo can tell the story of Intelligent Orchestration and evoke prismatic light through animation and characteristic motion.

### Animation Behavior

**Prism Icon:**
- Emphasis is always placed on the prism icon, which sets the stage.
- The icon animation can start from a small, singular point and expand outwards to a kaleidoscope of shapes, **or**
- Begin as a multifaceted prism and contract inward to its resolved state.

**Wordmark:**
- With a subtle pulse of light, the Cprime letters emerge from the prism icon.
- The motion mimics a soundwave expanding outward.
- Once the letters have formed the full logo, a ray of light beams across PRIME, creating the distinct curve of the P in the wordmark.

## VIDEO STYLE (Page 83)

Video is an opportunity to bring our illustration and photographic styles to life through motion. Using the same visual principles of both these styles, video content can include abstract, metaphorical footage, or focus on optimistic videos of humanity and natural, intelligent systems.

The pacing of our video style should always be fluid. While speed is an aspect of our business, this type of movement should be thoughtful and well-timed. The overall motion in of our videos should be calm, uplifting, and not overly fast-paced.

### Three Video Style Categories

#### ILLUMINATE
We use video clips that are light-filled and feature prismatic light effects. These videos showcase optimistic scenes of humanity and natural systems.

#### ORCHESTRATE
We use video clips that show metaphorical themes of Intelligent Orchestration. Clips can be from the natural world or CG.

#### ACCELERATE
We use light-filled clips that express ideas moving in real time and accelerating business growth.
