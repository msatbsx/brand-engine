# Aagee Typography

## Primary Typeface: Figtree

Figtree is the primary typeface for all presentations, brand marketing, and landing pages.

## Weights

Four weights are used to support flexible use cases:

| Weight | Use |
|--------|-----|
| Figtree Regular | Body copy |
| Figtree Medium | Body copy (secondary emphasis) |
| Figtree SemiBold | Headline copy |
| Figtree Bold | Headline copy (primary) |

Figtree Bold and SemiBold are used for headline copy.
Figtree Regular and Medium are used for body copy.

## Usage Rules

- Do not use weights outside the four approved weights
- Do not substitute Figtree with another typeface in branded materials
- Maintain hierarchy: Bold/SemiBold for titles, Regular/Medium for body text
