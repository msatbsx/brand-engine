# Aagee Brand Examples

Note: The Aagee Brand Guidelines (2025) include a "Brand in Use" section (pages 26–33) which contains visual examples of the brand applied across real contexts. This document describes those examples in text form.

## Approved Visual Combinations

### Logo on colour
- Black Aagee logo on Lime (#DDFB66) background — primary brand look
- Lime Aagee logo on Black (#111111) background — bold alternative
- Lime Aagee logo on Midnight Navy (#131F30) background — premium/dark alternative

### Logo in black & white
- Black logo on White — for print, documentation, and monochrome contexts
- White logo on Black — for print, documentation, and monochrome contexts

## Graphic Elements
The Aagee visual system uses bold geometric shapes derived from the logo's angular forms. Diagonal lines and triangular shapes at dynamic angles are characteristic of the brand's graphic language.

## Colour Usage in Practice
- Lime (#DDFB66) is the dominant accent against dark backgrounds
- Dark Grey (#1A1A1A) and Midnight Navy (#131F30) serve as primary backgrounds
- Mint (#9CEE95) and Sky (#83C6ED) are used sparingly as secondary accents
- Gradients using Lime, Mint, or Sky at 45° angle are permitted as background elements

## What Compliant Brand Work Looks Like
1. Uses only Figtree typeface in Regular, Medium, SemiBold, or Bold weights
2. Uses only approved palette colours
3. Places the logo only on high-contrast approved backgrounds
4. Maintains the logo clear space of 0.5X on all sides
5. Never uses Lime, Mint, or Sky coloured text on a white background
6. Never places the symbol logo above the wordmark
